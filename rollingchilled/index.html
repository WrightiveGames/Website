Rolling Chilled

Race the snowball downhill.

Coming December 14, 2026 to Google Play and the App Store

What it is

A snowball racing game you steer by tilting your phone. Roll off the start ramp, scoop up snow and skiers to grow your snowball, and race the pros down the mountain to the lodge. Your phone is the controller.

Screenshots

Five runs

  • Summit Run. A clear day on the original mountain. Race Axel Frost.
  • Pine Gully. Overcast and thick with pines. Race Juniper Vale.
  • Rocky Ridge. Steep, rocky and lit by the evening sun. Race Rocco Granite.
  • Moonlight. A cold, blue night run. Race Luna Nightfall.
  • Glacier Dawn. First light on the long blue ice. Race Aurora Vance.

Beat each run's race pro to unlock the next one. Every run has three difficulties, marked like ski trails: Green Circle, Blue Square and Black Diamond.

How it plays

  • Tilt your phone to steer. Everything you roll over makes the snowball bigger.
  • Skiers stick to it. Hit a pine or a rock and you lose some size.
  • JUMP to hop over trouble. Speed Boost unlocks when you win a Black Diamond race.
  • Earn snowflakes as you play and spend them on upgrades: higher jumps, faster recharges, a longer boost and a bigger snowball.

Make it yours

Spend snowflakes in the store on new looks.

  • Racer packs. Robots, Tourists and Aliens: every racer on the hill gets the new look.
  • Snowball skins. Nine skins in three packs of three: Sweet Treats (Peppermint, Sprinkle Donut, Mint Chip), Ball Games (Soccer, Basketball, Tennis) and Deep Space (Moon, Gas Giant, Lava Rock).
