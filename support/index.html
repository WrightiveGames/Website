Support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems? Email support@wrightivegames.com and include the game name, your phone model and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

Restoring Remove Ads

Open the game's Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you bought it with.

Deleting your data

Our games keep your progress on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it. See the privacy policy for what ad partners collect and how to opt out.
