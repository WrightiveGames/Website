About Us

We're a small family company, and the games are a family project.

Wrightive Games exists so a dad can build games alongside his kids. Ideas get tossed around at the dinner table, the kids test early builds and say exactly what's boring, and the best suggestions end up in the game.

How we work

  • Small on purpose. There's no big team or publisher. We make every game ourselves, from the first sketch to the last bug fix.
  • Played by the family first. If it isn't fun on a couch with the kids, it doesn't ship.
  • Short and satisfying. We make games you can enjoy in a minute or an hour.
  • Fair ads. Our games are free, with ads and one simple purchase to remove them.

Our games

Llits is two tilt games in one app, Drops and Roll. Fellstone is a quarry-building game set on real mountains.
