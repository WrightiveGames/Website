How it plays

Dig stone at your camp, roll boulders off the ledge and knock down the trees on the slope below. Every tree pays. Spend your coins on better tools, conveyors, cranes and a rock crusher, research upgrades in the lab, and sell rare minerals at the market.

Levels are built from real elevation data. Start on Mt Hood, then move on to Mt Rainier.
