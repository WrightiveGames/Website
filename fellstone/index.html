Fellstone

Roll the rock. Fell the forest. Build the quarry.

Coming soon to Google Play, the App Store and Steam

How it plays

Have you ever wanted to roll a big rock down a hill, with FellStone you can do it all day long! Dig up rocks by hand or with equipment and then launch them down the hills to knock over trees. Every tree knocked down earns you money that you can use to level up your character and your gear. Before too long you'll have an automating mining camp where you can just sit back and roll bigger and bigger boulders. Crush rocks into gravel for a steady source of income and sell rare gems and minerals at the market.

The levels are built using real elevation data from some of the most well known mountains.
