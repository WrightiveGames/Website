Llits

Steady hands for high scores.

Coming December 14, 2026 to Google Play and the App Store

What it is

A calm, steady-hands tilt game. Water lands on a glass on your screen. Tilt your phone gently to gather it into one puddle, and keep it from spilling over the edge. Your phone is the controller.

Screenshots

Four game modes

  • Glass. Calm. Learn to gather the water.
  • Beach. Wind gusts and wandering crabs.
  • Stroll. Take the glass for a walk. Every step sloshes.
  • Quake. Earthquakes crack the ground beneath.

Each mode has three intensities: Drizzle, Downpour and Deluge.

How it plays

  • Merge the water into one big puddle for up to a x10 score multiplier.
  • Spill too much and the run is over.
  • Unlock helpers: Edge, Tripod and Umbrella.
  • Real water physics, so every run plays differently.

Make it yours

Earn water drops as you play and spend them in the store on new looks.

  • Bright Water pack. See-through water in Lagoon, Lime, Sunset and Berry.
  • Solid Colors pack. Water you can't see through, in Milk, Ink, Cherry and Honey.
  • Seasons pack. Autumn, winter and spring backgrounds for every mode.
