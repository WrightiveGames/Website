Llits

Two tilt games in one app. Your phone is the controller.

Coming soon to Google Play

2 game modes Pick Drops or Roll from the main menu and switch any time.

Mode 1

Drops

Keep the water on the glass.

Tilt to gather scattered drops into one big puddle, and don't spill more than 15%. Wind, earthquakes and a bumpy walk through the park all try to make you.

  • Calm and careful
  • Goal: one puddle, minimal spill

Mode 2

Roll

Race a snowball down the mountain.

Launch a snowball, steer it with a tilt and scoop up every skier in your way. The more you catch, the faster you go. Beat each level's race pro to the lodge to unlock the next run.

  • Fast, big, chaotic
  • Goal: beat the pro to the lodge

Roll levels

Three runs are open from the start. Play to unlock more.
