Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. Applies to Llits, Fellstone and wrightivegames.com.

Who we are

Wrightive Games ("we") makes mobile games. Contact: support@wrightivegames.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks (which may include Unity Ads and Google AdMob). These partners may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Their use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies. If you are in the EU, UK or another region that requires it, we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the game's settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases

In-app purchases such as Remove Ads are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock it.

Crash and performance data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance statistics.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling our games. For questions or requests about your data, email support@wrightivegames.com.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date.
