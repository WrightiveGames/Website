Our games are made for everyone, including children

Our games are designed for players aged 9 and up, including children under 13. We built them to collect as little information as possible from everyone, and we add extra protections for children. We follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy, Apple's App Store rules for apps used by children, and, where they apply, the EU and UK GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code.

We do not ask any player for their name, email address, phone number, photo or precise location. Our games have no chat, no player profiles, and no way for players to share personal information with each other. Our games do not use analytics tools.

The age question

The first time you open one of our games, it asks for your birth month and year. We do not suggest an answer or encourage a particular one. The game uses your answer only to work out an age group (under 13, or 13 and older). Only the age group is kept, on your device. Your birth month and year are not saved and are never sent to us or anyone else. The question appears before any ads start.

  • Under 13: the game runs in child mode, described below.
  • 13 and older: the game still shows only non-personalized ads and collects the same limited information described in this policy.

If the wrong age was entered, a parent can reinstall the game, or clear its data, to answer the question again.

Child mode (players under 13)

  • Every ad request is flagged as child-directed (COPPA) to our ad partners. This tells them to treat the request as coming from a child and turns off interest-based advertising, retargeting and profiling.
  • Only ad networks certified under Google Play's Families Ads Program are used.
  • Purchases and links that leave the game are behind a parental gate that a young child is unlikely to pass.
  • We collect only the limited technical information COPPA allows without parental consent, used only to run the game, show contextual ads, limit how often ads appear, prevent fraud and keep the game working (what COPPA calls "support for internal operations").

What we collect and why

  • Game progress and settings (levels, scores, coins, sound and ad settings, age group), to save your game and run it properly.
  • Technical and ad information such as device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, an app-specific device identifier (such as the Android App Set ID or Apple IDFV, which cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps), and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. This is sent to our ad partners when an ad loads, to show a suitable ad, limit how often ads appear, confirm an ad was shown, and prevent fraud.
  • Purchase history (which item, and that it was paid for) from Google Play or the Apple App Store, to deliver what you bought, such as Remove Ads, and restore it later.
  • Crash logs and performance data provided by Google Play and Apple, to find and fix bugs. We do not share them with anyone else.

We do not collect the advertising ID (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA). Our games do not show Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt, because they do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Unity LevelPlay, which works with these ad networks, all certified for Google Play's Families Ads Program:

  • ironSource and Unity Ads, both operated by Unity Technologies (privacy policy)

If we add another ad network, such as Google AdMob, it will also be Families-certified, follow the same settings, and be listed here.

All ads in our games are non-personalized for every player, at every age. Ads are chosen based on things like the game you are playing and your general region, not on a profile of you or what you do in other apps. Under our settings, ad partners may use the technical information they receive only to deliver and measure the ad, limit how often it appears and prevent fraud, and not to build an advertising profile. Their own privacy policies also apply.

In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, we ask adults for consent where the law requires it before ad partners store or read information on the device, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings. Rewarded ads, such as watching an ad for extra coins, are always optional.

Purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases, such as Remove Ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store, and we never see your card or payment details. Purchase screens are behind a parental gate. Parents can also require approval for purchases with Google Family Link or Apple Ask to Buy.

Saved progress and backups

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run our own cloud save and cannot see your saved games. If your phone's own backup is turned on (Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad), your device may include the game's saved data in that backup. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies and your device settings, and we have no access to them. If we ever add our own cloud save, we will update this policy before it is turned on.

This website

wrightivegames.com does not ask for personal information and does not use cookies, analytics or ads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined in California law. We share information only with the service providers named in this policy (ad networks and app stores) for the purposes described here, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep information

  • Age group and settings on your device: until you delete the game or its data.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed to restore your purchases and as required by tax and accounting law.
  • Ad partners keep the technical information they receive under the retention rules in their own privacy policies.

How we protect information

We collect very little, and nothing we receive is linked to a name or contact details. Information sent by our games to our partners travels over encrypted connections. We keep a written information security program and review our partners' settings when we update the games.

Rights for parents

If your child plays our games, you can at any time ask to see the information we hold about your child, ask us to delete it, and tell us to stop collecting it.

To make a request, email support@wrightivegames.com with the game's name. Our games store your child's progress and settings only on the device, so deleting the game, or clearing its data, removes them. We will also pass deletion requests on to our ad partners where they hold information. We will confirm the request comes from the device's owner or the child's parent before acting, and will respond within 30 days. Because the information we collect from children is limited to what COPPA allows without parental consent, we do not ask parents for consent in advance. If that ever changes, we will get verifiable parental consent before collecting more.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, EEA, UK and U.S. states such as California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. Email support@wrightivegames.com and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests to run, secure and improve the games and to show non-personalized ads, on contract to provide purchases, and on consent where the law requires it for storing or reading information on your device.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the U.S. and other countries. Where required, they use approved safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change would let us collect more information from children, we will get verifiable parental consent first.
